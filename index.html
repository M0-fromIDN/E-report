About

IDN Boarding School Sentul E-Report is a small internal system for reporting facility issues. Teachers and staff can create, track, and manage reports easily from any device.

Services

Reporting

Create and submit facility issue reports with optional images.

Status Tracking

Monitor statuses like New, Processing, and Finished to follow progress.

Report Management

View details, edit and remove reports via the dashboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Click the "+ Add New Report" button, fill the form, optional image, then save.
